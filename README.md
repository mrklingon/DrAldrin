# DrAldrin
Orbital Mechanics on the Circuit Playground



* DrAldrin.js MakeCode version (also at https://makecode.com/_iRe1XehdvVHu)

* DrAldrin.py - CircuitPython version, copy to code.py on CPX
* bach.py - music routines, copy to CPX


  Simple "orbital rendevout" program. Orbital level defined by colors (red, orange, yellow, green, blue, indigo, violet) with the lowest (and fastest) at red. Boost to higher orbit pushing button A, and lower orbit pushing button B.

Goal is to match with your spaceship (fast moving red pixel) with the space station which is at the higher (and slower) orbit. Best approach is to raise your ship above the station, and let the station catch up with you. Success triggers music and flashing lights. 

Restart by pressing A+B together.
